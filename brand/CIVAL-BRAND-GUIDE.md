# Cival Systems Brand Guide

## Brand idea

Cival Systems is the calm engineering layer between trading ideas and deployed software. The brand should feel inspectable, exact, dark, and confident—never like a casino, signal room, or guaranteed-return pitch.

Primary line: **Trading systems, shipped as source.**

Supporting promise: **Inspect the code. Start in paper mode. Deploy on your own terms.**

## Logo system

- Primary monogram: `public/brand/cival-monogram-v1.svg`
- Horizontal wordmark: `public/brand/cival-wordmark-horizontal-v1.svg`
- Generated social avatar master: `public/brand/cival-social-avatar-master-v1.png`
- Minimum digital monogram size: 24 px
- Clear space: at least one-quarter of the monogram width on every side
- Do not recolor the mark purple, add coin imagery, compress it, rotate it, or place it on a light/noisy field

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cival Black | `#06110e` | Primary background |
| Deep System Green | `#0b1f19` | Panels and depth |
| Hyperliquid Green | `#4ade9f` | Primary mark, CTA, active state |
| Signal Mint | `#7fffc8` | Highlight and restrained glow |
| Circuit Green | `#174d3b` | Borders and diagrams |
| Cool Gray | `#9eaaa5` | Secondary copy |

Green is an interaction and identity color, not a profitability code. Losses, warnings, and destructive controls must remain semantically distinct.

## Typography

- Display: the site's existing editorial display face
- UI and body: the site's existing sans-serif system
- Data and labels: the site's existing monospaced face
- Social artwork fallback: a neutral Swiss grotesk such as Arial/Helvetica, uppercase with deliberate tracking

## Voice

Cival sounds like a senior systems engineer: direct, specific, and comfortable reporting limits.

Use:

- “historical simulation”
- “reference implementation”
- “source template”
- “paper mode first”
- exact versions, assumptions, fees, and failure states

Avoid:

- “guaranteed,” “zero risk,” “set and forget,” or “hedge fund in a box”
- unsupported performance claims
- urgency based on fabricated scarcity
- calling a scaffold “production ready” before it passes the release gate
